Our Marine Services

01

Main & Auxiliary Engine Spare

Genuine and equivalent spares for main and auxiliary engines, sourced against your engine maker, model and serial number.

Main Engine Spares

Cylinder liners, pistons and rings, cylinder heads, fuel injectors and nozzles, bearings, valves, turbocharger spares.

Auxiliary Engine & Generator Spares

Gensets parts, fuel and lube oil pumps, starters, gaskets, belts, filters and routine consumables.

Sourcing Details

Delivery

Coordinated delivery to the vessel at your port, with packing list and documentation.

Electrical & Electronic Equipment

Marine-grade electrical materials and electronic systems for vessel operations, maintenance and emergency requirements.

Marine Electrical Equipment

Electronic Systems & Components

Specification Support

Test & Verification

03

Ship Chandling & Supplies

Complete ship chandling for deck, galley and bonded requirements, supplied to your port and ETA.

Deck Stores

Ropes and mooring lines, shackles and chains, fenders, cleaning tools, brushes, tapes, PPE (helmets, gloves, goggles) and hardware.

Ship Provisions

Dry provisions, canned food, tea and coffee, biscuits, beverages, sauces and condiments, bottled water, and fresh produce on request.

Bonded Stores

Duty-free stores coordinated as per port and customs procedure, with the required documents and crew-entitlement limits.

Port Delivery

Consolidated delivery to the vessel with checklist-based receiving and signed delivery note.

04

Marine Consumables & Fluids

Marine paints, lubricants and potable water for planned maintenance and vessel operations.

Marine Paints

Jotun, Hempel, International, Sigma and other marine coating systems: primers, anti-fouling, topcoats, rollers and brushes.

Lubricating Oil

Engine oil, hydraulic oil, gear oil, grease and filters, from brands such as Shell, Mobil, TotalEnergies and Caterpillar / MAN-spec filters.

Fresh Water Supply

Potable fresh water to vessel tanks by barge or IBC, plus bottled drinking water and hose coordination at port.

Quality & Documents

LSA / FFA Inspection & Certification

Life-saving and fire-fighting equipment supply, servicing and inspection coordination to keep the vessel compliant.

Life-Saving Appliances (LSA)

Fire-Fighting Appliances (FFA)
